Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

AI Content Studio ("the tool") is a private tool used only by its owner for the owner's own channels and accounts (see Home). The only user whose data it accesses is the owner.

1. Platforms it connects to

YouTube (Google). Other platforms are added only through their official APIs and are listed with their status on Platform notes.

2. Data and permissions requested

Google / YouTube: OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. With it the tool reads the owner's channel identity (channel ID, title), the list and status of the owner's uploaded videos, and the channel's own settings; and it uploads the owner's videos and updates their metadata, captions, thumbnails and publish status.

The tool does not request access to contacts, email, location, payment information, or any other person's account.

3. How the data is used

  • To confirm that the authorized channel is exactly the intended channel before any write (a different channel stops the tool).
  • To upload, schedule and verify the owner's own videos.
  • To avoid duplicate uploads and to stay within the platform's API quota.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the owner's own channels.

4. Storage and protection

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the Windows Credential Manager, one entry per channel. They are never written to source code, repositories, logs or shared documents.
  • The tool keeps local operating records on the owner's computer: the verified channel ID and title, upload and schedule results (video IDs, times, status), API call counts for quota, and backup copies of the channel's own settings taken before changes.
  • The tool has no server of its own; nothing is sent to one.

5. Third-party AI services

Data received from Google APIs (channel identity, video lists, video status) is not sent to third-party AI services and is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.

Separately, the owner may use AI services to help write or translate the owner's own titles, descriptions and subtitles before upload. That text is the owner's own content, not data obtained from Google user data.

6. Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, rented or transferred to anyone. It is only sent back to the same platform as part of the owner's own uploads and edits.

7. Retention

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access or removes the channel from the tool. Local operating records are kept while the owner operates the channel and are deleted by the owner when no longer needed (see Data deletion).

8. Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked at any time at Google Account: Third-party connections. Deletion steps: Data deletion.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

AI Content Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Other platforms

Before another platform is connected, its permissions and data handling are added to Platform notes, and the rules on this page apply to it as well.
