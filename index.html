AI Content Studio

Last updated: 2026-11-24

AI Content Studio is a private, self-operated publishing tool. It is used only by its owner to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on the owner's own channels and accounts. It is not a public service: no other people can sign up, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

Content brands it operates

All are run by the same owner:

  • Same Game, Different Endings
  • Game Multiverse
  • Game Visual Lab
  • 게임TV (GameTV)
  • an AI game-play brand
  • Inside the Bite
  • content brands the owner adds later

What it does

  • Signs in to the owner's own platform accounts with the platform's official authorization (for YouTube: Google OAuth).
  • Uploads the owner's finished videos as private, sets title, description, captions and thumbnail, and sets the scheduled publish time.
  • Reads back the upload and schedule status from the platform to confirm the result.
